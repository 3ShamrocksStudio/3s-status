## 2. The loop
| Step | What the child sees | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **How are you?** | Home opens on a 5-icon feeling check, plus **"I'd rather not say"** | Optional. The heaviest choice opens Grown-up help in one tap. |
| 2. **Breathe together** (hero) | Milo grows and shrinks with the child: **in 4 · hold 2 · out 6**, 3 rounds, optional haptics | Framed as a strength skill ("calm power"), not as sadness |
| 3. **One small care skill** | Name the feeling · a drink of water · a stretch. About **1 in 4** opens it's a helper moment instead ("Milo's got butterflies. Breathe with him?"), where any help works | Helper moments never become the default and always hand back to the child |
| 4. **Today's brave move** | One small real-life quest ("say hi to a new kid"). Log **"I did it"** or **"maybe later"** | Both are fine. "Maybe later" costs nothing. |
| 5. **Grow together** | Milo and the child grow along 5 stages (Curled → Awake → Steady → Brave → Radiant) | **No streak counter. Bond never decays.** Growth comes from practice, not from logins. |

**Audience:** kids 6–12. Copy for 6–8 is read aloud and icon-led. Young teens are out of scope for v1. **Buyer:** the parent, who holds the account.

**Removed (Decision 1):** the AI chat, until a child-psychologist review (Common Sense Media advises against social AI companions for under-18s [G1]; separate parental consent is needed under COPPA to share a child's data with a third party [S10]). The on-device crisis scan and the scripted Milo replies stay.

## 3. Retention model (as the brief defines it)
| Horizon | "Retained" means | Lever (never guilt) |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Named Milo, finished one breathe-together, saw Milo respond | Onboarding ends in a felt calm win, not a feature tour |
| **D7** | Came back at least twice; reached stage 2 (Awake) | Soft growth + one new brave move; optional parent nudge ("want to breathe with Milo tonight?") |
| **D30** | Reached stage 3 (Steady); the child can name the breathe skill without the app | Skill-bridge copy, rotating quests, new expression art. No daily obligations. |

**Key metric: the share of sessions with a completed breathe or a named feeling.** Not DAU. If this share falls while opens rise, we've built a streak app by accident.

Secondary: brave moves logged per active kid per week (target ≥3, set the baseline in test 1); parent trial-to-paid and monthly churn (set after test 2).

**Benchmarks, as the brief cites them** (context only, not targets): youth MH apps lose most users within months (JAACAP 2025: 68/69 active in month 1, 19/69 by month 6 [G2]); MOST, a clinician-blended platform for ages 12–25, kept ~55% at 6 weeks [G3]; broader MH-app range cited as 0.5–28.6% at 6 weeks [G3]; Headspace D1 ~30% / D7 ~12% / D30 ~6% is a market-analyst figure, not youth-clinical [G4]. v1 cited median MH-app retention of 3.3% at day 30 (Baumel et al. 2019 [S15]).

## 7. Safety and legal (before any real child)
- **No clinical claims.** Never: therapy, treatment, anxiety, depression, diagnose. Use: calm, brave, practise, grow.
- **Crisis path:** heaviest feeling or Grown-up help → Milo calm: "Thanks for telling me. This one's for a grown-up." → full-screen card to show an adult → one tap to a verified helpline → emergency numbers → optional parent alert (no content shared).
- **Privacy:** parent account, verifiable parental consent (COPPA amendments, compliance due 22 Apr 2026 [S10]); Israel Amendment 13 in force 14 Aug 2025 [S29]; nickname + age band only; on-device by default; no third-party analytics (Apple Kids category, guideline 1.3 [S27]).
- **Reviews:** a child psychologist reads every line; a privacy lawyer checks the model. Both may cost money: **Dave's consent first.**
