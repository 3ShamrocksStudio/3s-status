## 1. Audit of Milo v0 (as built on your Mac)
| Area | Today (source) | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | A Tamagotchi-style "emotional-support companion" for ages 6–14. Milo has a "wobble", the child helps him, then Milo asks "how are YOU?" (`Milo/CONCEPT.md`) | The whole loop starts from distress. This is the pity loop you want out of. |
| Audience | "Vulnerable children (~ages 6–14)" (`Milo/AUDIT.md`) | 6 and 14 need different products. "At-risk" kids need clinicians, which brings a duty of care a one-person studio can't carry. |

## 4. Repositioning: hoppa, one brave move a day
**For kids:** "Milo doesn't do 'poor you'. Milo does 'let's go'."
**For parents:** "Confidence is a muscle. hoppa trains it in a few minutes a day, and most of it happens off the screen."
**Audience:** kids 8–12 (this also matches the age range of the outcome scale below). The parent is the account holder and the buyer.

### Core loop
| Step | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pick | 3 move cards each day, in sizes Easy, Brave or Legend. Themes: try something new, speak up, help someone, bounce back. | The kid chooses, so it feels like a challenge, not a chore |
| 2. Do | In real life, off the screen ("ask the waiter for ketchup", "say hi to a new kid") | The win happens in the world, not in the app |
| 3. Tell Milo | One tap: **Nailed it / Tried it / Not today**. All three earn credit, and "tried it" counts in full. | No failure state, no shame |
| 4. Win | Milo's Hyped face, a popsicle collectible, and a level-up every 5 moves | Something to show for it, with no streaks to lose |
| 5. Parent Win Card | Weekly and opt-in: which moves were done. It never shows mood data. | The parent sees the value, so the subscription renews |

### First session (≤3 minutes, a win before any feelings question)
1. The parent sets up the account (consent flow in §7) and hands over the phone.
2. Milo: "Yo. I'm Milo. I don't do 'poor you'. I do 'let's go'."
3. A practice move inside the app (teach Milo a high-five rhythm) gives the **first win in 30 seconds**: Hyped face and a first popsicle.
4. The kid picks today's real move and sets the time for a reminder.
5. A one-tap vibe check (5 weather icons). The lowest one opens Grown-up help (§7).

### Features
| Ship in MVP | Later | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Move deck (60 moves, Hebrew and English) · Tell-Milo log · collectibles and levels · Parent Win Card · vibe check with safety routing · Grown-up help button always visible · on-device data | Crew (two kids do a move together, parent-approved) · class packs for schools · a "comeback" bonus after a break | Open AI chat for kids · social feed · public leaderboards · streak punishment · ads |

### What "user success" means and how we measure it
| Layer | Metric | Target (proposal; set the baseline in test 1) |
|---|---|---|
| **North Star** | Brave moves logged per active kid per week | ≥3 |
| Habit | Share of kids with ≥1 move in 3 of their first 4 weeks; retention at day 30 | Beat the 3.3% day-30 benchmark [S15] by a wide margin |
| Kid outcome | Children's Hope Scale (6 items, validated for ages 8–16 [S18]) at day 0 and day 30, opt-in with parent consent. We report the change and claim no clinical effect. | Directional only |
| Parent value | Weekly "my kid tried something new" yes-rate · trial-to-paid conversion · monthly churn | Set after test 2 |
| Safety | 100% of lowest-vibe taps reach the Grown-up help screen within 1 tap, tested on every release · zero free text leaves the device | Hard gate |

### Business model (recurring first)
| Line | What the buyer gets | Price (**proposal, NEEDS YOUR EYES**) |
|---|---|---|
| **Family plan** | 1 parent and up to 3 kids, the full move deck, Win Cards, all collectibles | ₪29/month or ₪199/year after a 14-day free trial |
| Class pack (later) | A school or council licence for one class, with an anonymised class Win Card | After the data exists |
| Health funds (later) | Only with outcome data | Not before 2027 |
Pricing never goes in first-contact outreach.

## 7. Safety and legal (non-negotiable before any real child uses it)
### No clinical claims
- Never use "therapy, treatment, anxiety, depression, diagnose, mental-health app". Use "confidence, brave moves, wins, bounce back".
- List it in Apple's Kids category: no third-party analytics or ads, and a parental gate before links, settings and purchases (App Store guideline 1.3) [S27].

### Crisis escalation path
| Trigger | Step |
|---|---|
| Lowest vibe tapped, or the Grown-up help button (always visible) | **1.** Milo's Calm face: "Thanks for telling me. This one's for a grown-up. Let's get one." |
| | **2.** "Tell a grown-up you trust, now." A full-screen card the kid can show. |
| | **3.** One-tap call to **ERAN 1201** (24/7); ERAN chat, WhatsApp and SMS 08:00–02:00 [S11]. **Sahar** anonymous chat and WhatsApp [S12] (hours to confirm with Sahar; its pages disagree). |
| | **4.** "In danger right now? Call 101 (ambulance) or 100 (police)." These are standard Israeli emergency numbers; the clinical review re-checks every number. |
| | **5.** **Parent alert**, only if the parent switched it on at setup: "Your child asked for grown-up help in hoppa. Check in with them." No content is shared. |
| No free text in the MVP | So there is no chat to scan and no message that can leave the device |

### Privacy for minors
| Law | What applies | Our design |
|---|---|---|
| **COPPA** (US, under 13) | Verifiable parental consent. The 2025 amendments took effect 23 Jun 2025, with compliance due **22 Apr 2026**: separate consent for third-party disclosure, a written retention policy, an information-security programme; knowledge-based questions or text message are among the allowed consent methods [S10] | Parent account, no third-party sharing, a written retention policy |
| **GDPR-K** (Art. 8) | The age of digital consent is 13–16 depending on the member state (UK 13; Germany, Ireland and Netherlands 16; verify per country, because the source is inconsistent) [S28] | The parent consents for every child under 16 |
| **Israel, Amendment 13** | In force 14 Aug 2025 [S29]. There is no dedicated child-consent age; minors' consent goes through guardianship law. A privacy officer is required for large-scale processing of specially sensitive data [S30]. The privacy regulator's consent guidance, Apr 2026, tightens what counts as informed consent [S31] | Parent consent for every child; on-device by default keeps us small-scale |

**Data rules:** the kid profile is a first name or nickname plus an age band, nothing else. Everything stays on the device by default. The only optional cloud data is the parent's Win Card email. Delete on request. Auto-delete after 12 months of inactivity (proposal).

### Parental consent flow
1. The parent installs the app and creates an account. A neutral age question confirms the user is an adult.
2. A plain-language notice: what is stored, where, for how long, and who sees it (no one else).
3. Toggles. **Required:** create a child profile on this device. **Optional, each separate:** weekly Win Card email · alert me if my child taps Grown-up help · anonymous usage counts (first-party only).
4. Verifiable consent through a method the FTC allows [S10].
5. Hand-off: the kid's side opens, and a parental gate (a grown-up maths question) locks settings, links and purchases [S27].
