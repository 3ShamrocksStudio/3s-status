## 1. Concept audit and sharpened value proposition
| Question | Before (concept v2 [S4]) | Sharpened |
|---|---|---|
| **Who is the user?** | "Founders, investors and professionals, 25–50, who play casually" | **Sender:** someone who needs a first meeting with a senior person (founder, BD or sales lead, consultant, job-seeker). **Receiver:** a busy senior person who ignores pitches but will play 10 minutes. **Buyer:** organisers of founder meetups, VC community nights and conferences. |
| **What result do they get?** | "Meet someone worth knowing in ten minutes, without pitching" | Sender: **a reply, then a meeting.** Receiver: a fun interaction with no pitch, a time cap and a private opt-out. Organiser: **counted handshakes to show sponsors.** |
| **How is it measured?** | Games and handshakes counted | The funnel below, per sender and per Lounge |

### The funnel (each step is one metric)
| Step | Metric | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accept rate | Challenges opened and started ÷ challenges sent |
| 2 | Completion rate | Games finished ÷ games started |
| 3 | **Handshake rate** | Both players chose "Shake hands" ÷ games finished |
| 4 | **Meeting booked rate** | Calls booked through the sender's calendar link ÷ Handshakes |
| 5 | Meeting held | One-tap "Did you meet?" 7 days later |
| ⭐ | **North Star** | **Meetings booked per 100 challenges sent**, against the sender's own cold-message baseline |

## 2. Market and competitor scan
| Player | Model | What happened / signal | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn messages and cold email | Cold text | Average reply rate 0.45% (C-level 0.42%) in 7.53M emails [S3]. Other studies put it at 5–9% [S5], so benchmarks vary widely. | **Measure each sender's own baseline**; don't sell on an industry number |
| Lunchclub | AI one-to-one intros | $4M seed (a16z, 2019) and a $24M Series A (2020) [S6]; current status not confirmed in this scan | Intros alone don't stick; a reason to meet does |
| Shapr | Swipe-style professional matching | Raised $3M + $4M + $9.5M; closed in 2023 after an acquisition [S7] | Matching apps without a shared activity fade |
| Bumble Bizz | Swipe-style business matching | "Will slowly be phased out across all countries" [S8] | Same |
| Brella / Grip | Event matchmaking | Brella: custom pricing, no free plan [S9]. At InsureTech Connect 2017, 2,155 users booked 5,660 meetings (vendor case study) [S10]. | **Organisers pay for counted meetings.** That is the Lounge's job. |
| TechGames.xyz | Chess tournaments for tech people (London) | 10,000+ members, 200+ events, £10 suggested donation [S11] | Demand is proven; willingness to pay is low at the player level, so sell to organisers and sponsors |
| VC chess nights | Checkmate Club (Ridge Ventures, SF) [S12]; Founder's Chess Club [S13]; NY Tech Week "Chess Not Checkers" [S14] | VCs host chess for deal flow, with plain boards and no pairing, Handshake or report | **The VC host is a buyer or sponsor**: "The King's Table" |
| Chess.com | Online chess | 250M members (Feb 2026) [S15] | A large casual base already knows the game |
| Event attendees | Freeman 2025, 2,326 attendees surveyed | 54% network to find collaboration or commercial opportunities; 34% say networking feels "too salesy"; topic-led formats 51% vs no-topic 35% [S16] | **"Not salesy" is the hook.** The game is the topic. |

## 4. UX core loop
### Sender (1:1 challenge)
1. **Create a challenge** (30 s): pick 5+0 or 3+2 and write one line ("Who I am, why you"). The calendar link is optional.
2. **Share:** the sender pastes the link into their own LinkedIn, email or WhatsApp.
3. **Receiver opens:** sender card, "One game · 10 min max · no sign-up", Accept or Not now.
4. **Play:** a top-down board, a clock for each player, and **Rookie mode** (shows legal moves) for casual players.
5. **Handshake:** each side privately picks Yes or Skip. Details are revealed only on a mutual yes.
6. **Next step:** "Book 20 min" (the sender's calendar) or Rematch.
7. **Day 7:** "Did you meet?" (one tap) → the sender's funnel dashboard.

### Lounge (events, the paid line)
QR scan → pairing by self-rated level → game → Handshake → live "King of the Lounge" board on the big screen → **organiser report: counts only** (games, handshakes, meetings booked; no personal data).

### Success metrics
| Who | Success means | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sender | Got the meeting | Meetings booked per 100 challenges | ≥3× own baseline (proposal) |
| Receiver | Fun, no pressure | Completion rate · "Would play again" | ≥80% · ≥70% (proposal) |
| Organiser | Proof for sponsors | Handshakes per attendee · renewal | ≥1 per 2 attendees · renews next event (proposal) |
| R&K | Repeat use | Senders who send a 2nd challenge in 14 days | Set after the test |

### Business model (recurring first; **all prices are proposals, NEEDS YOUR EYES**)
| Line | What the buyer gets | Price | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lounge · Community plan** | Unlimited Lounges for one space or community, its branding on the boards, a monthly report | ₪490/month or ₪4,900/year [S4] | **Recurring** |
| Lounge · Event pass | One conference up to 3 days, big screen, setup | $1.5–5k (your Linear 3SHMRX-133 range) [S4] | One-off, converts to Community |
| Sponsor · The King's Table | Sponsor logo on boards, leaderboard and Handshake card | Bundled at first | Add-on |
| Sender · Pro | Unlimited challenges, the funnel dashboard, a custom challenge card | Set after test 1 | Recurring, later |
| Players | Challenges, games, Handshakes | Free | Growth loop |
No cash stakes, no ads for players.
