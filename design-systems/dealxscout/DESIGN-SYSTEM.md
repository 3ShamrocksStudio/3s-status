## 1. Essence
- **Mascot / host:** Scout, a dapper bloodhound detective (bowtie, magnifying glass).
- **Niche (one job):** AI for real-estate agents: listings, staging, speed-to-lead.
- **One-line promise:** "Sniffs out deals. Stages the listing. Catches the lead."
- Faceless page: the mascot is the profile picture and the recurring host; the content must genuinely teach.

## 2. Tokens
### Colour (from the brand kit; contrast measured)
| Token | Hex | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--base` Deep navy | `#0d1d31` | background; gradient up to `#274a73` | — |
| `--accent` Brass gold | `#d9a441` | the ONE spotlight keyword or the Scout stamp per frame, never a wash | 7.55:1 on base |
| `--text` Bone | `#f4ecdd` | headlines + body | 14.46:1 on base |
| `--label` Slate-blue | `#7f9bc0` | labels, index numbers, hairlines | 5.94:1 on base |
**Dark always:** no white-background posts. The accent is a spotlight, never a wash.

### Type (network standard)
- Headlines: **Space Grotesk** 600–700, tight tracking.
- Body / on-image sentences: **Inter** 400/500, generous line-height.
- Labels, index numbers: **Space Mono**, uppercase, wide-tracked.
Pairing: Grotesk = the hook, Inter = the information, Mono = the label. Don't mix the jobs.

### Spacing, radius, shadow, motion (kit rules + board proposals where the kit is silent)
- Canvas 1080×1350 (feed) / 1080×1920 (Reels); safe margin ~8% (≈86px) on every side; content occupies ~60% of the frame.
- Radius: sharp-to-small (0–8px) on panels; type-led, few boxes.
- Shadow: none on text; only a soft glow behind the one accent where the kit allows it.
- Motion (Reels): cut on the beat, kinetic captions, one move per idea; no flashing.
- Carousels: slide 1 = hook with the accent keyword, middle = one idea each, last = CTA.

## 3. Logo rules
- Files: `Media-Network/01-Scout/Logo/scout.png`, `scout-avatar.png` (1080 IG avatar), `Brand/logos/scout-a.jpg`; final picks in the Media-Network final-logos folder → `DEALXSCOUT/` (2 JPGs).
- The avatar (1080×1080) is the IG profile picture; inside posts the mascot is a small fixed mark: small brass Scout stamp ("case closed" seal) bottom corner of every post + handle; ~8% margins.
- Final logos are raster JPGs from an image generator: export transparent PNG/SVG before using the mark over gradients. Never box the mark on a contrasting tile; never recolour.
- No 3Shamrocks studio logo on posts (faceless network); studio credit only on the bio-link page footer.

## 4. Iconography
Thin line icons in the label colour; the accent colour only on the one key icon per frame. No emoji on images (emoji allowed in captions/bio).

## 5. Imagery
Type-driven. Exception: real before/after listing images inside staging reels. No faces, no stock, no emoji on image.

## 6. Voice and tone
Confident, not loud. Detective cadence: clue → finding → close. Dry, dapper wit. No hype, no exclamation spam.
| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "10X your listings with this AI hack!!!" | "Found it. Here's the comp they missed." |
| "Never miss a call for your business" | that is Fetch's lane; Scout says "never miss a *buyer* lead" |
**Disclosure (non-negotiable):** every affiliate post carries `#ad` / `#affiliate` or "I earn a small commission", plus IG's paid-partnership label where applicable. No financial, legal or medical advice. No invented results, earnings or follower numbers.

## 7. Components
Cover (hook + accent keyword + corner mark) · content slide (one idea, label top-left in Mono) · proof slide (real screenshot, anonymised) · CTA slide (handle + bio-link line) · Reel caption bar (Inter, cream on base at 80%).

## 8. Do / Don't
**Do**: dark base, one accent, heavy negative space, consistent margins, disclosed affiliates.
**Don't**: faces, stock photos, white backgrounds, more than one accent per frame, posting without Dave's GO.
