# Grants Shortlist — 3Shamrocks Studio
_Researched 27 Oct 2026. Every row below is from an official page opened today. "not published" = the page gives no figure. Nothing has been submitted._

**Bottom line:** Only one program is both a strong fit and open now: **IIA Tnufa, closing 9 Nov 2026**. You can file it twice in 12 months, once for Trashure-Hunters and once for SH✡MER, for up to ₪400k in total. The money comes as reimbursement of paid invoices from about Feb 2027 onward (decisions are due end of Jan 2027). Everything else is either a channel that opens later (municipal pilots, the National Insurance tech basket) or a long shot (BIRD, Horizon).
